# The Card Company — Product & Engineering Research Note

A clean, single-page, scroll-only editorial research note web application modeled after the layout of the Wispr Flow India research memo, re-themed to match **The Card Company's** luxury matte-black, brushed-silver, and 24K gold aesthetic.

Prepared by **Hemant Singh** (November 2026).

---

## 💎 Design Highlights

- **Outer Canvas:** Deep matte obsidian (`#080808`) with subtle radial dark charcoal ambient illumination and centered single-column reading focus.
- **macOS Editorial Window Frame:** Centered `max-w-[760px]` container styled like a sleek dark-mode macOS document window with red/yellow/green window dots, subtle silver-metallic border (`#262629`), and rich backdrop blur.
- **Reading Progress Indicator:** Razor-thin (`2px`) 24K gold progress bar pinned right under the window header that fills smoothly as the reader scrolls.
- **Editorial Typography:**
  - Headings: `Playfair Display` serif in brushed platinum (`#F5F5F7`) with italic 24K gold accents (`#D4AF37`).
  - Section Kickers: Uppercase monospace labels in muted metallic gold (`#C5A059`), `tracking-[0.18em]`.
  - Body: High-legibility `Plus Jakarta Sans` in soft silver-grey (`#B8B8BE`) with generous line-height (`1.8`).
- **Data & Segment Visualizations:**
  - 2x2 responsive grid for Field Research findings (`#151518` cards with gold hairline accents).
  - Customer Segment comparison cards with brushed silver & 24K gold top borders.
  - Hover-highlighted Strategy Priority and Campaign Idea editorial blocks.
  - 4-week vertical execution timeline with gold milestone dots.
